Trip Tracker · New App UI · Design only

“Horizon” design system
for iOS, Android & Web

A calm, light-gray canvas with big white rounded cards, one confident electric blue for action, and four cinematic dark “context surfaces” that tell you what you’re looking at — Night Sky for travel, Ember for money, Slate for insights, Dusk for night mode & celebrations. Everything below is lifted from your reference set (Iconly cards, wallet, charts, dusk dark-mode, empty states, bottom-menu styles) and adapted to Trip Tracker’s real screens.

7 · iOS vs Android — what actually differs

Titles & backiOS: large collapsing title, “‹ Trips” back chip, edge-swipe back. Android: 28px title on the left, arrow back, predictive-back animation.
SheetsiOS: 36px radius, grabber, detents (½ / full). Android: 28px radius, drag handle, full-width actions.
NaviOS: floating glass dock with raised centre FAB. Android: flat M3 bar with indicator pill + extended FAB above it.
ControlsiOS toggles (51×31) & wheel date picker. Android M3 switch with thumb icon & calendar-dialog picker. Same colours either way.
Status / systemiOS Dynamic Island safe-area 54px, home-indicator 34px. Android edge-to-edge, punch-hole camera, 3-button/gesture bar aware.
Haptics & soundiOS light impact on tab change, success notification on “Settled”. Android tick on toggle, confirm on settle. (Setting already exists.)

8 · Responsive rules for Flutter Web

< 600 · PhoneSingle column, floating dock, sheets from bottom.
600–1023 · TabletNavigation rail (84px), 2-column cards, sheets become centred 520px dialogs.
≥ 1024 · Desktop248px sidebar, content max-width 1120, optional 340px right rail (Balances, Activity). Expense detail opens in a side panel instead of a sheet.
≥ 1440 · WideContent centred, trip workspace gains the right rail permanently.

9 · Motion & delight

Flight arcOn Trips home the plane glides along the arc to today’s progress when the card first appears (600ms, decel).
Number rollTotals and balances count up/odometer-roll when data changes (200ms).
DockActive pill slides between tabs (spring); FAB rotates 90° into × while the quick-add sheet is open.
StampWhen a trip is fully settled the passport stamp “thumps” onto the card (scale 1.3→1, rotate −12°).
